## 1. Evidence board / crime wall

Files: `01-evidence-board/a-desk-and-wall.webp`, `01-evidence-board/b-corkboard-lamp.png`

**A — wall above a desk.** Warm sepia. An anglepoise lamp top-right throws a hot spot onto the
upper-right of the board; the rest falls off into brown shadow. The board is *full*: black-and-white
portraits, landscape photos, typed sheets, sticky notes, a city map underneath it all. A web of red
string radiates from a few hubs, with red pin heads at every node. A hand-lettered card in red marker
("WHO IS THE KILLER?") is the single loudest element. Below, the desk continues the story: stacked
files, a coffee mug, a magnifying glass on a folder labelled "CASE 37".

**B — framed corkboard under a pendant lamp.** Darker and cleaner. A shallow dish lamp hangs centred
above; a defined cone of light falls on the board and fades to near-black brick at the edges. A real
cork surface in a wooden frame. Neat mugshot grid in the middle, maps and newspaper pages at the
sides, yellow sticky notes, red string converging on the faces. A hand-written "EVIDENCE" header.

**What works**
- One light source, and the darkness around it. The light *is* the composition.
- Warm cork/paper against a cold dark room: warmth = information, dark = unknown.
- Red string reads instantly as "connection"; pins are red dots that mark every node.
- Variety of paper: polaroids, mugshots, newsprint, sticky notes, typed sheets, maps.
- Handwriting on the board (header, questions) makes it feel personal.
- Things overlap and sit slightly crooked.

**What doesn't fit**
- A (photo-real, AI-generated sepia) is over-saturated orange and its clutter would bury
  a playable board; our board must stay readable with 30+ items.
- Pure brown cork everywhere would fight the cold slate/amber palette.

**What CASEFILE will do**
- Board: a framed board inset into the dark wall (B), warm cork-toned surface inside a dark wooden frame,
  slate wall around it. Keep the hanging lamp and a defined light cone (B) instead of a soft glow.
- Pins: red pin heads on every pinned item; red string threads with a slight sag.
- Handwritten board header "EVIDENCE" and optional handwritten question cards
  (players can already add notes; style them as index cards / sticky notes).
- Mixed paper styles for pinned items: polaroid for photos, mugshot frame for people,
  typed sheet for documents, yellow sticky note for player notes.
- Desk below the board (A) — evidence tray as the desk edge with a few props (mug, magnifier) at the corners on desktop.
- Keep it cleaner than A: clutter is decoration around the edges, never on the play area.
